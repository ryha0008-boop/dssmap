# The Dead Sea Scrolls (dssmap)

Live site: https://ryha0008-boop.github.io/dssmap/

Three linked, self-contained web pages:

- **[Guide](dead-sea-scrolls.html)**: what the scrolls are, how they were found, and the most important ones, with photographs.
- **[Catalogue](dead-sea-scrolls-catalogue.html)**: every manuscript, searchable, with its surviving text in the original language and in English.
- **[The Scrolls and the Scriptures](dead-sea-scrolls-book.html)**: a reading edition arranged by subject, verse by verse, beside passages from the Bible, the Quran, the Talmud, the Ethiopian Bible and other traditions.
